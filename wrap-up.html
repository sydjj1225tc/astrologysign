You are more than your signs ✨

A spark of curiosity, not a prediction

Spark is here to help you explore yourself and find a lighthearted way to connect. These astrology readings are not fortune-telling. They do not predict your future, define your personality, or decide who you can become.

Keep what feels useful 🌷

Use the descriptions as prompts for self-discovery. Notice what resonates, question what does not, and leave the rest behind. Your experiences, choices, and values tell a fuller story than any sign. You are allowed to grow beyond a description—or never identify with it at all.

Start a conversation 💬

At a gathering, Spark can be an icebreaker or a playful small-talk topic. Try asking, “Does your sign sound like you?” or “What helps you feel comfortable meeting new people?” Stay curious about the person’s answer rather than assuming you know them from their sign.

Show up as yourself

You do not need to fit a type or perform a personality to belong. Be warm in your own way, respect your boundaries, and let connection happen at your pace. You bring more to a room than a chart could ever describe.
